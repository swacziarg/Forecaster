# Canada Liberal Comeback v2

This private-draft bundle contains the exact YES-token hourly series for Polymarket market `517586`, its retained raw-history assembly, and the market metadata used by `canada-liberal-comeback-v2`.

The CSV is the application-facing normalized artifact. It contains 2,723 hourly observations from `2025-01-06T19:00:00Z` through `2025-04-30T05:00:00Z`, with no internal missing buckets. The market was not open for the full history window represented by the broader research period, so `fullMarketLifetime` is deliberately `false`.

This bundle is a local playable draft input. It is not a published study or approval of the September 12 content date.
