# 2026 Best Picture study: editorial review record

Status: implementation draft awaiting independent editorial approval

## Candidate set

The implementation carries five eligible candidates into review: the National Board of Review Best Film announcement, Critics Choice Best Picture win, split Golden Globes picture results, Oscar nominations announcement, and Producers Guild top theatrical-picture win.

Each event has a primary first-party record, a declared information cutoff, a pre-reveal factual claim, a preregistered direction, a mechanism, competing explanations and retrospective interpretation. The Golden Globes split and Academy nomination field are intentionally labeled ambiguous rather than treating every precursor as unconditionally positive. The Academy nomination time is anchored to the official 5:30 a.m. PT announcement.

## Review still required

Do not change the study status from `editorial-review` to `approved` or `published` until an independent editor:

- selects a reproducible licensed or public coverage archive and records unique-publisher counts;
- adds independent contemporary corroboration for each first-party record;
- scores every rubric dimension with written justification;
- decides whether Critics Choice and Golden Globes are too redundant to rank separately;
- checks the exact first-public timestamp of every award result;
- confirms that no event was retained because of the observed market move;
- signs the inclusion decisions with reviewer identity and approval timestamp;
- captures immutable source snapshots or content hashes.

The current five-event UI is therefore a generalization and calculation proof, not a claim that editorial publication criteria are complete.
