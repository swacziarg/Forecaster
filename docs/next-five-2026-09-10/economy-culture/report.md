# Next-five candidate review: economy and technology

## Recommendation

The September 2024 Fed 50+ bp contract is ready for independent editorial review. The retained exact YES series covers July 25–September 18, and the strict audit window begins after the July jobs report with 1,129 complete hourly buckets and no missing hours. The five proposed cards use the August 5 market shock, August CPI, Jackson Hole, September jobs, and September CPI. The FOMC decision is a separate reveal-only ending.

The GPT-5 date market is a strong technology storyline with five dated OpenAI releases and a clean August 7, 2025 conclusion, but it is not data-ready. The exact date outcome token and same-contract history must be retrieved before any market-response claim, ranking, or schedule proposal. The source-backed narrative is suitable for review as a conditional candidate.

## Fed evidence and caveats

The exact market asks whether the Fed would lower the target range by at least 50 basis points after the September 2024 meeting. Polymarket records the YES outcome as settled, and the Federal Reserve’s September 18 statement is the resolution source. The retained dataset is `public/data/fed-2024/polymarket-hourly.csv`; its manifest records 1,323 hourly observations, while the research audit trims the usable continuous window to August 2 21:00 UTC through September 18 21:00 UTC.

The August 5 card is sourced to PBS/AP’s account of the global rout and links the market shock to both growth fears and the yen carry trade. It should be described as a plausible pressure on the Fed contract, with attribution caveated. The BLS and Federal Reserve sources provide exact publication timestamps for the four later releases. The five cards contain both supportive and adverse information and avoid the July jobs report’s pre-audit timing problem.

The prior audit reports a 51.75 percentage-point daily-median drawdown from August 5 to September 12 and a 26-point rebound from September 12 to September 15. Those are descriptive series facts, not proof that any one headline caused the move. Registration still needs a fresh five-window calculation and overlap review using the full retained packet.

## GPT-5 evidence and caveats

The event page describes a multi-outcome date market and shows August 7, 2025 as the resolved date. The proposed cards are official OpenAI releases: GPT-4.5 on February 27, GPT-4.1 on April 14, o3/o4-mini on April 16, o3-pro availability on June 10, and ChatGPT agent on July 17. These are concrete, publicly dated developments that could plausibly change expectations about sequencing and launch timing.

The candidate remains conditional because the exact August 7 outcome token and historical prices are not in the retained repository packet. Until they are retrieved, the file intentionally contains no invented probability move, score, or causal attribution. OpenAI’s GPT-5 announcement should be retained as the separate resolution ending.
