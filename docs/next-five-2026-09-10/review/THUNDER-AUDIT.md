# Thunder audit: withdrawn from recommendation

The owner questioned the four-card tie. The lead re-examined raw observations and independently re-fetched 96 hourly points for May 17–20. All 96 exactly match the retained provider response.

The principal problem is the news anchor: Denver Game 7 is measured at the May 19 11:48:17 UTC article update, after the market already repriced. Raw samples rose from 43.5% at May 18 19:00:06 UTC to 59.0% at 22:00:05 UTC. That 15.5-point difference illustrates the missed movement; it is not a newly approved event score or a causal estimate. The draft reports 0 because its reference and stabilized windows both fall after much of the jump.

The current four responses are -0.5, 0, 0 and +0.5 percentage points, so the inclusive one-point anchor rule groups them. Almost all retained prices lie on half-point increments, and window medians can produce quarter-point changes. The rule and sampled feed explain the tidy numbers, but do not excuse the late anchor.

Thunder is withdrawn as a scheduling recommendation. Its existing draft and scores remain for audit, not release. Reconsideration requires first-announcement/result anchors supported by contemporaneous evidence, event-appropriate before/after windows, and a fresh editorial review. No cutoff should be chosen for the size of its resulting move. The same article-update risk should be checked in the other sports draft before approval. The schedule remains unchanged.
