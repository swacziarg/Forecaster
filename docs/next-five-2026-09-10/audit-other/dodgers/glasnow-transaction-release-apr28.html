Dodgers recall Noah Davis

April 29th, 2025

LOS ANGELES – The Los Angeles Dodgers recalled right-handed pitcher Noah Davis and placed right-handed pitcher Tyler Glasnow on the injured list with right shoulder inflammation.

Davis, 27, made three appearances in his first stint with the Dodgers, going 0-1 with a 10.13 ERA. He has been in the Major Leagues parts of four seasons with the Colorado Rockies (2022-24) and Dodgers (2025), going 0-5 with a 7.83 ERA in 21 games. The Southern California native was originally drafted by the Reds in the 11th round of the 2018 First Year Player Draft out of UC Santa Barbara.

Glasnow, 31, allowed two runs in one inning yesterday. He has made five starts this season, going 1-0 with a 4.50 ERA in 18 innings. In two seasons with the Dodgers, he is 10-6 with a 3.61 ERA in 27 starts. He is in his 10th season, and he is a combined 40-33 with a 3.83 ERA in 154 career games. The Southern California native was drafted by the Pirates in the fifth round of the 2011 First Year Player Draft out of Hart High School.
