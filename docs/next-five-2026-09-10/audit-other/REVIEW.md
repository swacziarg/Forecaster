## Sensitivity limits

The Fed and corrected Dodgers tie groups remain the same at ±1h. Reordering two IDs inside a tied group does not count as a changed ranking. Trump’s debate/Iowa pair splits at −1h. NYC’s two endorsement cards split at +1h.

Broader scenarios are diagnostics, not alternative timestamps chosen to improve scores. Moving the unresolved AOC publication bound six hours earlier produces +2.55 pp instead of +0.20; that is a reason to disclose uncertainty, not claim the endorsement caused that larger move. Moving Glasnow’s bound three hours earlier produces +0.5 rather than 0 and splits the small-response group.

For Dodgers, a separate six-hour pre-first-play baseline gives NLDS +11, pennant +2.5, Ohtani 0 and seventh loss −1 pp. This supports the larger playoff movement without relying on a reference that includes in-game trading.
