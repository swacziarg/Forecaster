5. **November 2, 2024 — final Iowa poll.** Card text: “A final Iowa poll puts Harris ahead of Trump, 47% to 44%.” Measurement cutoff: `2024-11-03T00:00:00Z`, a broadcast upper bound. Sources: [CNN transcript](https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/cnr/date/2024-11-02/segment/08) and [Reuters report](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/harris-tops-trump-in-latest-iowa-poll-marking-turnaround-des-moines-register-survey-3698510). This is a strong candidate for a five-card story because it has a clear positive shock, multiple negative reversals, an exact retained contract, and a reproducible score packet. The two editorial costs are the repeated Butler event and the approximate debate cutoff.

2. **June 24, 2025 — Cuomo concedes the Democratic primary.** Card text: “Andrew Cuomo concedes the Democratic primary to Zohran Mamdani.” Measurement cutoff: `2025-06-25T04:00:00Z`, a broadcast upper bound. Sources: [CNN transcript](https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/lcl/date/2025-06-24/segment/01) and [FOX5 report](https://www.fox5ny.com/election/zohran-mamdani-lead-cuomo-concedes/). Observed stabilized response: **+33.80 pp**; delayed cumulative: **+33.775 pp**. Returns were already rolling in, so this is a primary-night breakthrough cluster rather than an isolated concession effect.

3. **July 14, 2025 — Cuomo continues as an independent.** Card text: “Andrew Cuomo announces he will continue running for mayor as an independent.” Publication/modification time: `2025-07-14T19:03:00Z`. Observed stabilized response: **-0.925 pp**; delayed cumulative: **-1.975 pp**. The option had been discussed earlier, so the announcement confirmed an anticipated path.

4. **September 14, 2025 — Hochul endorsement.** Card text: “Governor Kathy Hochul endorses Mamdani for New York City mayor.” Publication/modification time: `2025-09-15T00:31:00Z`. Source: [NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/arts/news/2025/09/15/hochul-endorses-mamdani-in-nyc-mayor-s-race). The endorsement followed public discussions and other September campaign news.

5. **September 28, 2025 — Adams exits.** Card text: “Mayor Eric Adams ends his re-election campaign.” Publication/modification time: `2025-09-28T17:18:00Z`. Sources: [NY1](https://ny1.com/nyc/all-boroughs/politics/2025/09/28/eric-adams-drops-out), [court record](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-nyed-1_25-cv-04558/pdf/USCOURTS-nyed-1_25-cv-04558-0.pdf), and [archived post](https://perma.cc/B8BX-S9BU). Withdrawal had been publicly speculated about, and another major anti-Mamdani candidate remained.

The retained tie groups are primary breakthrough; Hochul endorsement; AOC endorsement; and Cuomo independent with Adams exit. This edition is data-ready and the cards are consequential even where measured movement is small.
